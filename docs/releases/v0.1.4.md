Sessions come back typeable after a long outage, and attaching inside tmux
on a Mac no longer types a color reply into the shell.

- **Owner again after a long outage.** A client that lost its connection
  for longer than the 30 s ownership grace came back as a viewer of a
  session nobody owned, so everything typed was dropped until Ctrl-T.
  Resuming now claims the session back when this client was its owner
  and nobody has taken it since. A client that took over meanwhile keeps
  it. Both ends need this release; against an older server a resume
  behaves as before. ([#8](https://github.com/rjyo/osewa/issues/8))
- **No stray color reply under tmux on macOS.** `osewa attach` inside tmux
  on a Mac could leave `4;36;rgb:0000/afaf/8787` at the remote prompt: a
  pty read cut the terminal's color replies on an ESC, which went out as
  a key and the rest of the reply as text. A reply cut that way now waits
  for the next read. ([#7](https://github.com/rjyo/osewa/issues/7))

This release interoperates with v0.1.3 (`p0-s1`).
